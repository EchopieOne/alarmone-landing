Alarm One FAQ

Alarm One requires iOS 26 or later. City alarms need Pro; importing your own audio needs Pro and iOS 26.1 or later. Visit support for setup help.

How is Alarm One different from the built-in Clock app?

Alarm One adds work/rest rotations, every-N-weeks, monthly and yearly repeats, specific dates, alarms in another city's time, skip next, alarm groups, delete after ringing, custom sounds, calendar views, widgets and iCloud sync.

Can I set an iPhone alarm for a specific date?

Yes. In Alarm One, create an alarm that rings once, enable Specified Date, and select the future date and time. The regular Clock alarm editor offers times and weekday repeats.

How do I automatically delete alarms after they ring?

Create a temporary alarm in Alarm One and enable “Delete this alarm after it rings.” This removes that Alarm One alarm after use; it does not delete entries from Apple’s Clock app.

Can I import a custom alarm sound without GarageBand?

Yes. With Alarm One Pro on iOS 26.1 or later, import a playable MP3, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or M4A file from Files, select it in the sound picker, and save the alarm.

How do world clock alarms work?

Choose a target city and set its local time in Alarm One. The app calculates the local equivalent using the city’s time zone and daylight saving rules.

Can Alarm One handle rotating shifts?

Yes. Choose Workday → Rotation, set the first work date, and enter the consecutive work and rest day counts. For changing start times or irregular rosters, use separate specified-date alarms.

Can I skip just the next alarm without turning it off?

Yes. In Alarm One you can skip the next occurrence of a repeating alarm, for example for a public holiday, and it rings again as normal after that.

Can I set a monthly or yearly alarm?

Yes. Alarm One repeats alarms monthly (on a day of the month) or yearly (on a date), as well as on workdays, weekdays, weekends or once.

Can I turn off a group of alarms at once?

Yes. Put alarms in a group, then turn the whole group on or off, or skip it, in one step.

Do Alarm One alarms sync to my iPad and Mac?

Yes. Alarm One syncs alarms with iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

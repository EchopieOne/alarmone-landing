Welcome to Alarm One!

Thank you for choosing our alarm app! This guide will help you get the most out of your alarm experience and troubleshoot common issues.

What You Need

  • iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch with iOS 26.0 or later
  • Internet connection for some smart features
  • Sound enabled on your device

Amazing Features You'll Love

Smart Alarm Options

  • One-time alarms: Perfect for specific events
  • Daily alarms: Wake up at the same time every day
  • Custom schedules: Set alarms for any pattern you need

Smart Features

  • Reliable ringing: Uses Apple's system alarm technology
  • Live updates: See alarm status on your lock screen

Common Questions & Solutions

My Alarm Didn't Ring

  1. Check your volume: Make sure your device isn't on silent
  2. Do Not Disturb: Check if the moon icon is showing in your status bar
  3. App permissions: Open the app and allow any permission requests
  4. Device restart: Sometimes restarting your phone helps

Alarms Not Showing Correct Days

  1. Internet connection: Smart features may need internet to sync updates
  2. App update: Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store

App Keeps Closing

  1. Update iOS: Go to Settings > General > Software Update
  2. Reinstall app: Delete and download again from App Store
  3. Storage space: Check you have enough space on your device

Tips for Best Experience

  • Keep the app updated for the latest features
  • Allow notifications so you don't miss alarms
  • Use headphones if you need private alarm sounds
  • Set multiple alarms for important events

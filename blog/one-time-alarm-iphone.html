What is a one-time alarm?

A one-time alarm rings once, then removes itself from your active alarm list. It is different from a daily alarm, a recurring alarm, or a reminder notification. The point is simple: it should wake or alert you once, then get out of the way.

Best uses for one-time alarms

Naps

Set a 25-minute or 90-minute alarm without creating another permanent item.

Flights and travel

Create early alarms for one travel day, then let them disappear after the trip starts.

Medication, cooking, meetings

Use an alarm when the alert really needs to interrupt you, not just sit in Notification Center.

Why cleanup matters

Old alarms make future alarms harder to trust. When your list is full of past flights, old school pickups, stale nap timers, and random backup alarms, it becomes harder to see what will actually ring next.

The clean-list rule

If an alarm exists for one moment only, it should not become permanent UI debt.
